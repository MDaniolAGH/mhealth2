## The map

Three lanes: the **client** (the clinic, which puts the app on the market and is responsible for it), **you**, and the **outside world** (regulators, notified bodies, app stores). On the left, the **technical file**: the records that launching depends on. Along the side, **accountability**: being able to show what you did and why.

		{ text: 'Report a personal-data breach to the data protection authority within 72 hours', bin: 'always', why: 'A duty of the controller under GDPR Article 33, for any app that processes personal data.' },
