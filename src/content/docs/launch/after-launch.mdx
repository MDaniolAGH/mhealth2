## Incidents

Something will go wrong: a bug, a lost phone, a leaked log. What matters is that you know **in advance** who does what.

- **If it is a medical device:** post-market surveillance is a legal duty (MDR Articles 83–86), and serious incidents must be reported (Article 87).
- **For every app with personal data:** the controller must report a personal-data breach to the supervisory authority without undue delay and, where feasible, within **72 hours** of becoming aware of it, unless the breach is unlikely to put people at risk (GDPR Article 33). Sometimes the people affected must be told as well. In Poland, that authority is UODO.

Write down who notices, who decides, who reports, and who can be reached on a Friday evening. Then update the risk file: every incident is a hazard you now know about.

## Updates never stop

<Predict id="drift" question="You change nothing in the Mood Tracker for a year. Does it keep working as it is?">

Probably not. In a year: Android and iOS get new versions, and the stores raise their requirements (Google Play requires apps to target a recent Android version, a rule that moves every year). Libraries get security fixes, some of which you must take. TestFlight builds expire after 90 days. An app with nobody looking after it slowly stops working, or becomes unsafe. Budget for upkeep in the business case.

</Predict>

- **Platforms:** new Android and iOS versions, new store rules, a yearly rise in the Android target API level that Google Play requires.
- **Libraries (SOUP):** follow security advisories, update on purpose, run the full gate and tests, update the SOUP list.
- **Your own toolchain:** pinned Flutter and Python versions, so that a build in six months is the same build.

## Measuring without leaking

Product teams measure everything: activation, retention, funnels, A/B tests, session recordings. With health data, most of the usual tools are a problem: they send what happens in the app to a third party. Measure what you need for the decision, with the least data: counts on your own server, aggregated, with no content of entries, agreed with the controller. A/B testing a health-related feature on participants starts to look like research, and may need the same approvals as research.

## The end of the study

The study ends in July. Decide early, with the controller:
