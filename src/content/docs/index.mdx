## Part 1 · The AI SDLC: from idea to merge

The guide follows **one feature**, an About screen for the Mood Tracker you took over, from a sketch to a merged pull request. At every stage it shows who does the work, what goes in, what comes out, and what checks it. Each page has something to try, a few questions to check yourself, and a task for your own project.
