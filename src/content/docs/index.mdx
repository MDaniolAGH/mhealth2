## One feature, ten stages

The guide follows **one feature**, an About screen for the Mood Tracker you took over, from a client's sketch to a merged pull request. At every stage it shows who does the work, what goes in, what comes out, and what checks it. Each page has something to try and a few questions to check yourself.

## How to use this guide

	</Card>
