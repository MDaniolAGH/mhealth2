## Part 1 · The AI SDLC: from idea to merge

The guide follows **one feature**, an About screen for the Mood Tracker you took over, from a sketch to a merged pull request. At every stage it shows who does the work, what goes in, what comes out, and what checks it. Each page has something to try, a few questions to check yourself, and a task for your own project.

## How to use this guide

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Before W3" icon="open-book">
		Read the map and sections 1 to 4 of Part 1. They build on W2: how a model works, context, and your pair's `AGENTS.md`.
	</Card>
	<Card title="During the labs" icon="laptop">
		Keep section 5 open while you work on your client's brief. Each stage says what it should leave behind.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Predict, then check" icon="pencil">
		Boxes marked **Predict first** ask you to commit to an answer before you see ours. Your answers stay in your browser only.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Synthetic data only" icon="warning">
		Everything here uses the Mood Tracker's synthetic data. Never put real personal or health data into the app, or into an agent's context.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
