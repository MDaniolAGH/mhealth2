## How to use this guide

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Follow the lifecycle" icon="open-book">
		Discover, build, launch: the parts follow the course from W1 to W6. The course ends with one final project; nothing is handed in week by week. Each page starts with a TL;DR and ends with a task for that project.
	</Card>
	<Card title="During the labs" icon="laptop">
		Keep the map of the part you are working on open. Each stage says what it adds to your final project.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Predict, then check" icon="pencil">
		Boxes marked **Predict first** ask for your own answer before you see ours. Your answers stay in your browser only.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Synthetic data only" icon="warning">
		Everything here uses the Mood Tracker's synthetic data. Never put real personal or health data into the app, or into an agent's context.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
